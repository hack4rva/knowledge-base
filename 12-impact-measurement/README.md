# Impact & Measurement

KPIs, metrics, data collection, and impact reporting for the Richmond Civic Hackathon.

---

## 🎯 Dual-Stakeholder Success Framework

The hackathon serves two primary institutional stakeholders with distinct success metrics:

1. **City of Richmond** - Civic impact and public accountability
2. **CoStar Group** - Corporate ROI and brand value

Success requires measurable outcomes for both.

## 📊 Dual-Stakeholder Success Metrics (KPIs)

### City of Richmond KPIs (Civic Impact)

| Metric Category | KPI | Target (Year 1) | Measurement Method |
|-----------------|-----|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Impact Efficacy** | % of projects deemed "Pilot-Ready" | 25-30% | Implementation Task Force evaluation |
| | Projects entering pilot phase | 2-3 projects | Tracking of pilot deployments |
| | Projects fully deployed | 1-2 projects | City IT/department records |
| **Community Engagement** | Total participants | 150-200 | Registration data |
| | Female/non-binary participation | 40% | Demographic survey (optional) |
| | Racial diversity | Match Richmond demographics | Demographic survey (optional) |
| | Zip code diversity | 10+ Richmond zip codes | Registration data |
| **Financial/Operational** | Cost per implemented solution | <$5,000 | Budget / # implemented |
| | Total event cost | <$50,000 | Financial records |
| **Perception** | Participant satisfaction (NPS) | >8.0/10 | Post-event survey |
| | City staff satisfaction | >4.0/5 | City department survey |
| | Citizen satisfaction with new services | >75% positive | User surveys (6 mo post) |
| **Long-Term Adoption** | Solutions receiving micro-grants | 3-5 | Grant program tracking |
| | Implementation Champions assigned | 5+ | City commitment records |

## 📈 Data Collection Methods

### Pre-Event Data

**Registration Data:**
- Total registrations
- Registration timeline (early vs. late)
- Demographics (optional self-reported)
- Skills and experience levels
- Team formation preferences
- Employer/school affiliations

**Outreach Tracking:**
- Email open and click rates
- Social media engagement metrics
- Website traffic and conversion
- Sponsorship commitment timeline

---

### During Event Data

**Real-Time Metrics:**
- Actual attendance vs. registration
- Check-in timeline
- Meal claim rates (actual vs. projected)
- WiFi usage patterns
- Mentor session attendance
- Workshop/talk participation

**Observation Data:**
- Team formation success
- Participant engagement levels
- Diversity of teams
- Use of resources and mentors
- Problem areas or friction points

**Social Media Monitoring:**
- Hashtag usage and reach
- Sentiment analysis
- Real-time engagement
- User-generated content

---

## 📋 Survey Instruments

### Participant Post-Event Survey

**Sections:**
1. **Overall Experience**
   - Satisfaction rating (1-10)
   - Would you recommend? (NPS)
   - Likelihood to return next year

2. **Event Logistics**
   - Registration process
   - Venue and facilities
   - Food and beverage
   - Technology infrastructure
   - Schedule and timing

3. **Learning & Growth**
   - Skills developed
   - Learning objectives met
   - Networking value
   - Mentor helpfulness

4. **Inclusion & Belonging**
   - Felt welcomed and included
   - Team formation process
   - Code of Conduct awareness
   - Accessibility accommodations

5. **Challenge & Impact**
   - Challenge quality and clarity
   - Civic impact potential
   - Resources and support
   - Implementation likelihood

6. **Demographics** (Optional)
   - Gender identity
   - Race/ethnicity
   - Age range
   - First-time hackathon participant?

7. **Open Feedback**
   - What worked well?
   - What could improve?
   - Other comments

---

### Sponsor Feedback Survey

**Sections:**
1. **Value Delivery**
   - Benefits received vs. expected
   - Brand visibility satisfaction
   - Networking opportunities
   - Talent pipeline value

2. **Partnership Experience**
   - Communication quality
   - Logistics and coordination
   - Flexibility and responsiveness

3. **ROI Assessment**
   - Likelihood to sponsor Year 2
   - Recommended tier for Year 2
   - Referral to other sponsors

4. **Open Feedback**
   - Highlights and successes
   - Suggestions for improvement

---

### City Staff / Implementation Champion Survey

**Sections:**
1. **Challenge Design**
   - Problem relevance
   - Solution quality
   - Implementation feasibility

2. **Partnership Value**
   - Volunteer team collaboration
   - Mentorship support
   - City resource requirements

3. **Implementation Pathway**
   - Clarity of next steps
   - Support and resources
   - Timeline expectations

4. **Departmental Impact**
   - Potential for adoption
   - Staff engagement
   - Strategic alignment

## 📊 Impact Reports

### Immediate Impact Report (2 Weeks Post-Event)

**Audience:** Sponsors, City leadership, team

**Contents:**
- Event summary and highlights
- Attendance and participation metrics
- Challenge submissions and winners
- Photos and media coverage
- Initial feedback highlights
- Thank you and recognition

---

### Quarterly Impact Report (3, 6, 9, 12 Months)

**Audience:** Sponsors, City, Implementation Champions

**Contents:**
- Implementation progress updates
- Pilot program status
- Micro-grant utilization
- Deployed solutions showcase
- Media and community engagement
- Lessons learned
- Year 2 planning updates

---

### Annual Impact Report (12 Months Post-Event)

**Audience:** Public, sponsors, partners, future participants

**Contents:**
- Year 1 comprehensive review
- Full KPI dashboard (City + CoStar + Community)
- Success stories in depth
- Financial transparency
- Lessons learned and improvements
- Year 2 vision and plans
- Call to action for participation/sponsorship

---

## 🎯 Continuous Improvement Framework

### Retrospective Process

**Timing:** 2-4 weeks post-event

**Participants:** Core team, key volunteers, select participants

**Framework:**
1. **What went well?** (Keep doing)
2. **What didn't go well?** (Stop doing)
3. **What should we try?** (Start doing)

**Documentation:** Lessons learned document for knowledge base

---

### A/B Testing Opportunities (Year 2+)

- Challenge types (code vs. data vs. design)
- Team formation methods
- Judging criteria weights
- Marketing messages and channels
- Schedule variations
- Prize structures
