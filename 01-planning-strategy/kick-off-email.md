# Richmond Civic Hackathon - Core Team Kickoff

Hey Alex, Crystal, Tom, and Nick,

I'm thrilled to officially kick off our work together as the core team for the Richmond Civic Hackathon. I've talked to each of you individually, but this email marks the beginning of something I think could become a defining moment for our city, and for the people who make things here.

## The Vision

**The vision is simple but powerful: bring Richmond's technologists together to solve a real problem for our city.**

We've all built valuable skills in our professional lives (skills that make us marketable, employable, and in demand). But what happens when we aim that same talent at a civic challenge that matters to our neighbors, our neighborhoods, and our shared future? That's the premise of this initiative.

This isn't about solving a "boring problem." It's about creating a venue where creativity and technical ability meet civic purpose. I want it to feel energizing, the kind of thing people talk about afterward at work, at school, at church, at the pool. The kind of thing that makes Richmond feel like a city that's alive with talent and possibility.

Teams from all across Richmond (VCU, CarMax, Capital One, local startups, civic orgs) will come together for a two-day sprint in spring 2026. The Mayor's already on board and ready to help shape it. The groundwork is laid; now it's our job to bring it to life.

## What's Next

### 1. Join the Code & Cloud Con Kickoff

If you can, please be there (you don't need a ticket). We'll host a workshop where, together as a community, we'll choose the theme and domain for the hackathon. The Mayor will make the final call, but this will be our moment to shape the ideas that go into that decision. Tom and I will co-lead that session.

### 2. Commit to a Regular Rhythm

Let's start with short bi-weekly check-ins through December, moving to weekly in the run-up to the event.

### 3. Find Your Lane

**Crystal:** you've already mentioned helping shape logistics and promotion, and that's huge.

**Alex and Tom:** partnerships and sponsorships will be critical; we'll need both your experience and networks.

**Nick:** venue, funding, and operational connections will be invaluable.

**Me:** I'll keep driving the vision, filling the gaps, and ensuring we're aligned with the city's expectations.

But most importantly, I want each of you to think about what you feel called to do. We're a small team right now — that's a strength — and there's real room for creative ownership.

## Final Thoughts

Lastly, I just want to say: this matters to me deeply. I moved back to Richmond because I believe this is one of the best places in the world to live as a human being. With the right mix of talent, leadership, and civic spirit, I think we can show the city (and the next generation) what happens when engineers decide to build for their community.

**Let's make something people will remember.**

Ford
