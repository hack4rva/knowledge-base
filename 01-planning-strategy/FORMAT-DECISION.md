## 🎯 The Format

### Traditional Overnight Marathon Hackathon
This is a **classic overnight hackathon** scaled for civic impact:

**Friday 9 AM → Saturday 6 PM (33 continuous hours)**
- Participants hack continuously with venue open 24/7
- Food, caffeine, and mentors available throughout
- Overnight encouraged but not required (come/go as needed)
- Security and staff on-site entire time

**Sunday Morning: Virtual Judging (9 AM - 12 PM)**
- Judges review submissions via Zoom
- Top 10 teams selected

**Sunday Afternoon: Awards Banquet (3-7 PM)**
- Public celebration with 350+ attendees
- Top 10 teams present
- Mayor Avula presents awards
- Family-friendly dinner and networking

### Target Scale
✅ **500 participants** (Richmond's largest hackathon ever)  
✅ **33 hours of hacking** (authentic overnight experience)  
✅ **$12.5K in prizes** (serious incentive)  
✅ **Public celebration** (community engagement)

### Challenge 2: Team Coordination
**Issue:** Teams split across Friday/Saturday

**Solutions:**
- Require 1 member present each participating day
- Provide collaboration tools (Slack, GitHub)
- Friday evening "handoff" session
- Saturday morning "merge" session for new members

**Action:** Design team formation process for split attendance

### Challenge 3: Volunteer Staffing
**Issue:** 3 days = more volunteer hours

**Solutions:**
- Shift system (no one works all 3 days)
- Core staff: 2-day max
