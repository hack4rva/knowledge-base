# Hack for RVA - Richmond's Civic Innovation Weekend

**March 6-8, 2026 | CoStar Headquarters**

---

## What We're Building

**Hack for RVA brings together Richmond's entrepreneurs, inventors, and technologists to solve real problems for the City of Richmond—and inspires everyone to rethink what's possible when we join together.**

This isn't just another hackathon. It's a weekend where Richmond's brightest minds work alongside the Mayor's Office to build solutions that will actually be implemented. Where a CarMax engineer teams up with a VCU student and a community organizer to reimagine how our city works. Where we prove that Richmond has the talent and the heart to solve our own challenges.

---

## The Opportunity

Richmond is at an inflection point. We have:

✅ **The Talent** - Thousands of technologists at CoStar, CarMax, Capital One, Dominion, and VCU  
✅ **The Leadership** - Mayor Danny Avula with a clear action plan for Richmond's future  
✅ **The Challenges** - Real civic problems that need solving, from climate resilience to small business support  
✅ **The Moment** - A city ready to show what public-private partnership can achieve

But we've never channeled this energy toward civic good. **Until now.**

## The Challenges - Focused on Economic Growth

**Strategic Focus: Mayor's Pillar 4 - A Thriving Economy**

We're tackling Richmond's economic development challenges, aligned with Mayor Avula's vision for a thriving economy:

### 🌳 Tree Equity Scorecard
*Help Richmond achieve RVAgreen 2050 goals*  
Build a GIS dashboard showing tree canopy coverage, heat vulnerability, and where to plant next.

### 🏛️ One-Click RVA Reporting  
*Modernize how residents connect with City Hall*  
Redesign 311 reporting so issues get to the right department, fast.

### 💼 Richmond Business Navigator  
*Support Richmond's entrepreneurs*  
Help new businesses understand zoning, licenses, and support programs in one place.

### 💧 Flood Risk Mapper  
*Build climate resilience*  
Show residents their flood risk and what the City is doing about it.

### 🚶‍♀️ Green Space Access Map  
*Ensure every Richmonder can reach quality parks*  
Track progress toward 10-minute access for all neighborhoods.

**Plus 3-5 more challenges** across housing, transit, equity, and economic development.

## What Makes This Different

### 1. Real Implementation
Every challenge has a City department champion ready to adopt winning solutions. This isn't just demos—it's civic procurement.

### 2. Open Source by Design
All civic solutions default to open source licensing so the City can actually use them. We've pre-cleared the legal pathway.

### 3. 6-Month Support
Winners get mentorship from CoStar and City staff, plus micro-grants ($1K-5K) to help pilot their solutions.

### 4. Built for Everyone
Not just for coders. We need designers, data analysts, policy experts, and community organizers. If you care about Richmond, there's a role for you.

### 5. A 10-Year Vision
This isn't a one-off. It's the first annual civic innovation weekend that becomes a Richmond tradition.
