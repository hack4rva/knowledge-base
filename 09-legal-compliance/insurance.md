## 📋 Required Insurance Coverage

### 1. General Liability Insurance

**What It Covers:**
- Bodily injury to participants, volunteers, or guests
- Property damage at venue
- Personal injury claims (libel, slander)
- Medical expenses for event-related injuries

**Required Coverage:**
- **Minimum:** $1,000,000 per occurrence
- **Aggregate:** $2,000,000
- **Event Dates:** March 6-8, 2026
- **Location:** CoStar Headquarters, Richmond, VA

**Estimated Cost:** $500-$800 for single event

### 2. Cyber Liability Insurance

**What It Covers:**
- Data breaches during registration or event
- Participant information theft or loss
- Network security failures
- Privacy violations

**Required Coverage:**
- **Minimum:** $500,000
- **Covers:** Participant data, payment information (if any), project code

**Estimated Cost:** $600-$800 for single event

### 3. Professional Liability (E&O) - Optional but Recommended

**What It Covers:**
- Errors or omissions in event organization
- Negligent advice from mentors
- Failure to deliver promised services
- Sponsor/vendor disputes

**Recommended Coverage:**
- **Minimum:** $500,000

**Estimated Cost:** $400-$600 for single event

## 🚨 Insurance Gaps & Risks

### What Insurance Does NOT Cover

**Participant Personal Property:**
- Lost, stolen, or damaged laptops/phones
- Personal belongings
- **Mitigation:** Recommend participants insure own devices

**Participant Health Issues:**
- Pre-existing conditions
- Illness or injury not caused by event negligence
- **Mitigation:** Participants should have own health insurance

**Cyber Attacks on Participant Projects:**
- Security breaches of hackathon projects themselves
- **Mitigation:** Cyber insurance covers event data, not individual projects

**Prize Disputes:**
- Disagreements over prize distribution
- **Mitigation:** Clear terms in Terms & Conditions
