# Hack for RVA - Code of Conduct

**Adapted from Major League Hacking (MLH) Code of Conduct**

> **TL;DR:** Be respectful. Harassment and abuse are never tolerated. If you are in a situation that makes you uncomfortable at Hack for RVA, please report it using the procedures in this document.

---

## Our Commitment

Hack for RVA is committed to creating an inclusive, welcoming, and safe environment for all participants. We believe that every person has the right to participate in a respectful and harassment-free event, regardless of:

- Gender identity and expression
- Age
- Sexual orientation
- Disability (visible or invisible)
- Physical appearance or body size
- Race or ethnicity
- Religion or lack thereof
- Social class or economic status
- Veteran status
- Citizenship status
- Technical skill level or experience

---

## What We Do NOT Tolerate

Harassment includes but is not limited to:

- Offensive verbal or written comments related to any protected characteristic
- Sexual images, language, or advances in any context
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Harassing photography or recording without consent
- Sustained disruption of talks, workshops, or other events
- Inappropriate physical contact or unwelcome sexual attention
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior
- Doxxing, brigading, or sharing personal information without consent
- Creating a sexualized environment through images, activities, clothing, or materials

**If what you're doing is making someone feel uncomfortable, that counts as harassment and is enough reason to stop doing it.**

---

## Who This Applies To

This Code of Conduct applies to **everyone** participating in Hack for RVA:

- Participants (hackers)
- Sponsors and sponsor representatives
- Judges
- Mentors
- Volunteers
- Organizers
- City of Richmond staff
- CoStar employees
- Family members and guests (Sunday public event)
- Online interactions related to the event (Discord, social media, etc.)

---

## Scope & Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies at:

- **Event Venue:** CoStar Headquarters (all days: Friday, Saturday, Sunday)
- **Event-Related Gatherings:** Any social events, meals, or gatherings
- **Online Spaces:** Discord server, social media, email, video calls
- **Transportation:** Any event-provided or coordinated travel

### If Someone Violates This Code

Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to **comply immediately**.

If a participant engages in harassing behavior, organizers may take any action deemed appropriate, including:

1. Warning the offender
2. Temporary removal from event spaces
3. Expulsion from the event with no refund or compensation
4. Reporting to CoStar security or local law enforcement
5. Ban from future Hack for RVA events

## Our Responsibilities

### Organizers Will:

- Clearly communicate this Code of Conduct to all participants
- Post this Code of Conduct prominently at the venue
- Include acceptance in registration process
- Train all staff and volunteers on enforcement procedures
- Respond to reports within 15 minutes during event hours
- Take appropriate action to maintain a safe environment
- Provide support resources for anyone who needs them

### Organizers Will NOT:

- Dismiss or minimize reports of harassment
- Publicly identify reporters or those who filed reports (unless required by law)
- Retaliate against anyone who files a report in good faith
- Allow harassment to continue once reported

---

## Expected Behavior

We expect all participants to:

### Be Respectful
- Treat everyone with dignity and respect
- Use people's preferred names and pronouns
- Respect people's boundaries (physical and emotional)
- Be considerate of different skill levels and backgrounds

### Be Inclusive
- Welcome newcomers and help them feel comfortable
- Use inclusive language
- Respect diverse perspectives and experiences
- Make space for others to contribute

### Be Collaborative
- Work together toward civic solutions
- Give credit where credit is due
- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Provide constructive feedback kindly

### Be Professional
- Focus on building solutions for Richmond
- Represent yourself, your team, and the event positively
- Follow venue rules and staff instructions
- Take care of the space and equipment

## Intellectual Property & Project Conduct

### Open Source Commitment
All civic challenge solutions default to open source licensing (MIT/BSD) to enable City of Richmond implementation. See [ip-licensing.md](./ip-licensing.md) for details.

### Fair Play
- Build your own projects; don't plagiarize others' work
- Give credit for code, designs, or ideas you use
- Follow the rules of any APIs, datasets, or tools you use
- Respect licensing terms of open source software

## COVID-19 & Health Safety

While we are an in-person event, we respect individual health choices:

- Masks are welcome and supported (but not required)
- Maintain respectful personal space
- Stay home if you're feeling unwell
- Wash hands frequently; sanitizer provided
- Respect others' comfort levels with physical proximity
