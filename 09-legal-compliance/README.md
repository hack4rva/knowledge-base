## 🛡️ Insurance Requirements

### General Liability (GL) Coverage
**Purpose:** Protect against bodily injury or property damage claims

**Coverage Details:**
- Bodily injury per occurrence
- Property damage per occurrence
- Personal and advertising injury
- Medical expense coverage

**Cost:** $100 - $500 for single event
**Budget Allocation:** $500 - $1,500 (with buffer)

### Cyber Liability Insurance (MANDATORY)
**Purpose:** Protect against technology and data breach risks

**Why Mandatory:**
- Heavy reliance on technology
- Intensive network usage
- Collection of PII during registration
- Large number of devices and users
- Potential for data exposure

**Coverage Includes:**

**First-Party Coverage:**
- Forensic investigations
- Data recovery costs
- Crisis management
- Notification costs
- Credit monitoring for affected individuals

**Third-Party Coverage:**
- Legal defense fees
- Regulatory fines
- Lawsuits from participants/partners
- Privacy law violation defense

**Cost:** Included in $500 - $1,500 total insurance budget

### Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance
**Purpose:** Protect against professional liability in post-event support

**When Needed:**
- If providing ongoing development support
- If offering incubation/acceleration program
- If advising on implementation

**Consideration:** Strongly recommended for Year 1+

## 📜 Intellectual Property Framework

### The Critical Challenge
**Problem:** Default IP ownership = inventors, but:
- City employment contracts may claim IP
- CoStar employment contracts may claim IP
- University policies may claim student IP
- Unclear ownership = implementation paralysis

**Solution:** Proactive "Civic Open Source Default" policy

### "Civic Open Source Default" Policy

**Core Requirement:**
All participants working on civic challenges intended for City adoption must:
1. Register under event terms releasing prototypes under Open Source license
2. Choose permissive license (MIT or BSD recommended)
3. Grant City irrevocable right to use, modify, and implement

**CoStar Employee Waiver:**
CoStar must issue pre-approved internal waiver confirming:
- Code contributed uses public domain or permissively licensed IP, OR
- CoStar irrevocably grants Open Source license to City for prototype

**Benefits:**
- Eliminates procurement battles
- Fast-tracks City adoption
- Enables community contribution
- Allows team members to showcase work in portfolios

### Pre-Event Legal Checkpoint (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

**Week 1 Actions:**
- [ ] Identify City department that will maintain winning code (likely IT or CAO's office)
- [ ] Get City Legal counsel to review OSS policy template
- [ ] Obtain written approval from City Legal
- [ ] Finalize participant IP declaration form
- [ ] Secure CoStar internal waiver for employees

**Without these, implementation probability plummets.**

## 📋 Terms & Conditions

### Code of Conduct (Required)

**Core Principles:**
- Respectful and inclusive behavior
- No harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Professional communication
- Reporting procedures for violations
- Consequences for violations

**Reference:** Adapt from MLH Code of Conduct or similar

### Participant Eligibility

**Who Can Participate:**
- Age 16+ (or 18+ if alcohol served)
- No restriction by location (but prioritize Richmond area)
- All skill levels welcome
- Students, professionals, community members

**Who Cannot Participate:**
- Event organizers (conflict of interest)
- Judges (conflict of interest)
- Potentially: City employees working on same challenge they designed (case-by-case)

### Prize Eligibility and Tax Implications

**Legal Requirements:**
- Winners may need to provide Tax ID (SSN/EIN) for prizes over $600
- Winners responsible for any tax liability
- International participants may have additional restrictions
- Age restrictions for certain prizes

**Prize Distribution:**
- Teams split prize unless otherwise agreed
- Payment method (check, direct deposit, gift card)
- Timeline for distribution (30-60 days post-event)

### Liability Waivers

**Required Waivers:**
- Assumption of risk
- Release of liability for injury
- Medical emergency consent
- Property damage disclaimer

### Photo/Video Release

**Media Consent:**
- Photography and videography during event
- Use in marketing and promotional materials
- Social media posting
- News media coverage

**Opt-Out Option:** Provide opt-out mechanism for those who don't consent

## 🔒 Data Privacy & Compliance

### Personal Information Collection

**Data Collected:**
- Name, email, phone
- Emergency contact
- School/employer (optional)
- Demographic info (optional, for D&I metrics)
- Dietary restrictions
- Photo/video (if consented)

**Data Usage:**
- Event logistics and communication
- Emergency contact purposes
- Diversity metrics (anonymized)
- Post-event follow-up

**Data Protection:**
- Secure storage
- Limited access (core team only)
- Not sold or shared with third parties
- Deleted after retention period (1-2 years)

### GDPR / CCPA Considerations

**Not likely to apply but consider:**
- If any EU participants, GDPR applies
- If California residents, CCPA may apply
- Privacy policy should address these

## ⚠️ Risk Management

### Potential Legal Risks

**High Risk:**
- Data breach or PII exposure → **Cyber insurance**
- Personal injury on premises → **GL insurance**
- IP disputes blocking implementation → **OSS policy**

**Medium Risk:**
- Code of Conduct violations → **Clear policies + enforcement**
- Prize distribution issues → **Clear terms + tax compliance**
- Participant contract disputes → **Terms & Conditions**

**Low Risk:**
- Food-borne illness → **Licensed catering + GL insurance**
- Property damage → **GL insurance + security protocols**

### Crisis Communication Plan

**In Event of Legal Issue:**
1. Assess situation immediately
2. Contact insurance provider if applicable
3. Consult legal counsel
4. Communicate transparently with affected parties
5. Document everything
6. Follow established procedures

**Pre-Approved Talking Points:**
- Frame partnership as authentic, cost-efficient resource mobilization
- Focus on solving defined community problems
- Emphasize proper legal and insurance protections in place
