## 💰 Budget Overview

### Know Your Costs (MLH Best Practice)

**Before you can begin asking for money, get a sense of how much cash you need to pull off your hackathon.**

This budget determines:
- The sponsorship requirement for your hackathon
- The logistical load of your organizing team
- The tools and services you have at your disposal

**Start with an Excel/Sheets budget tracker** that includes:
- Categories of expenses essential to creating the best hacker experience
- Estimated costs based on your expected number of attendees
- Unit costs, quantities, and claim rates
- Revenue projections and cash flow timeline

**Critical:** Build in a buffer of **$1,000 - $3,000** depending on event size. In MLH's experience, organizers will always need this extra money. If you don't, you now have a head start for next year!

## 💵 Detailed Budget Model (200 Participants)

### Food & Beverage

**MLH Best Practice:** Food is one of the most significant costs and contributors to overall hacker experience at any hackathon. Everyone expects high quality food that respects their dietary restrictions.

**Key Considerations:**
1. **Order enough while not being wasteful**
   - Use QR code check-ins to track actual attendance at each meal
   - Order less food as hackathon progresses (claim rates drop)
   
2. **Donate excess food** if possible
   - Talk to nearby shelter or student organization
   - Must do this quickly to keep food safe for consumption
   
3. **Dietary restrictions are mandatory**
   - Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher options required
   - Collect dietary info during registration
   - Label all food clearly

**HackUTD Claim Rate Data** (via QR code scans at check-in/meals):
- **Day 1 Lunch:** ~90% (highest attendance)
- **Day 1 Dinner:** ~80%
- **Midnight Snack:** ~70%
- **Day 2 Breakfast:** ~30% (lowest attendance)
- **Day 2 Lunch:** ~60%

*Note: These don't include food for sponsors, volunteers, mentors, organizers. Order fixed additional amounts for those groups.*

**Our Event (Friday 12pm-10pm, In-Person):**

| Item | Unit Cost | Quantity | Claim Rate | Actual Orders | Total |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|---------------|-------|
| Lunch (12-1pm) | $9.00 | 200 | 90% | 180 meals | $1,620 |
| Afternoon Snacks (3-5pm) | $3.00 | 200 | 60% | 120 units | $360 |
| Dinner (6-7:30pm) | $12.00 | 200 | 85% | 170 meals | $2,040 |
| Evening Snacks (9-10pm) | $3.00 | 200 | 50% | 100 units | $300 |
| Coffee/Beverages (All Day) | $2.00 | 200 | 80% | 160 servings | $320 |
| **F&B Subtotal** | | | | | **$4,640** |

*Weighted claim rate strategy: Order heavy for peak times (lunch, dinner), lighter for low-attendance times*

**Additional MLH Guidance:**
- Don't order white t-shirts (frequently see-through depending on quality)
- Budget $8-10/person/meal aligns with our $9-12 estimates ✅
- Consider dietary needs as 20-30% of orders (vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free)

## 💡 Cost Savings Strategies

### Maximize In-Kind Contributions
- CoStar venue (already secured): $8,000 value
- Food sponsor instead of cash: $3,000-$5,000 savings
- Tech platform donations (GitHub, AWS): $1,000-$2,000
- Volunteer labor instead of paid staff: $5,000+ value

### Smart F&B Management
- Weighted claim rate ordering (avoid over-ordering)
- Local catering (avoid high-end event catering)
- Simple, crowd-pleasing menu
- Bulk ordering discounts

### Marketing Efficiency
- Leverage City's communication channels (free earned media)
- RVA Tech website and networks (free)
- Organic social media (free)
- Focus paid ads only if surplus budget

## 🎯 Financial Controls

### Approval Process
- **<$500:** Single approver (Treasurer/Finance Lead)
- **$500-$2,000:** Two approvers required
- **>$2,000:** Full team review and approval

### Expense Documentation
- All expenses require invoice/receipt
- Track against budget categories
- Monthly budget review meetings
- Variance analysis for >10% differences

### Bank Account
- Dedicated checking account for hackathon
- Two signatories required for checks
- Online banking access for treasurer
- Monthly reconciliation
