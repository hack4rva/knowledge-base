## 🎯 Leading Your Team (MLH Best Practices)

It's vital to get your team onto the same page for your hackathon so you're all working as a collective team rather than individual teams with different goals.

### Project Management

**Key Principles:**
- **Single Point of Responsibility:** One person is responsible for each team, managing the day-to-day tasks within it
- **Transparency:** All tasks tracked for the entire team to see
- **Accountability:** Follow up with each team lead to get status updates and keep them accountable

**Recommended Approach:**
1. **Choose a project management tool** and stick with it
   - Options: Trello, Notion, Asana
   - Current tool: **[TBD - Team to decide]**
   
2. **Assign team leads to manage tasks** with clear deadlines
   - Each team owns specific boards/sections
   - Regular check-ins with lead organizers
   
3. **Avoid over-engineering** your planning process
   - Sometimes the simplest solutions are easiest to adopt
   - Tool doesn't matter as much as consistency

### Team Management

**As you integrate new team members:**
- There's a bunch for them to learn about team dynamics and their role
- **Team building activities:** Try out mini-events you're considering having at the event
- Get the team more comfortable with each other before event day
- Create clear onboarding process and documentation

### Staying Organized

Working towards organizing any event becomes difficult if there's any communication gap in your team. If your team is working remotely (or partially remote), it's even more important to have a system in place.

**Tools We're Using:**

  - Status updates at team meetings

**Critical:** Pick your tools and **stick to them**. Consistency > Complexity.

## 📅 Meeting Cadence

### Phase 1: Planning (Oct 2025 - Dec 2025)
- **Frequency:** Bi-weekly
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Focus:** Strategic planning, partnership building
- **Format:** 
  - Each team lead reports on progress
  - Blockers identified and resolved
  - Next 2 weeks priorities assigned

### Phase 2: Execution (Jan 2026 - March 2026)
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Duration:** 45-60 minutes
- **Focus:** Tactical execution, problem-solving
- **Format:**
  - Stand-up style updates (5 min per team)
  - Deep dives on critical issues
  - Weekly sprint planning

### Phase 3: Post-Event (April 2026+)
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Focus:** Implementation support, lessons learned, Year 2 planning
- **Format:**
  - Implementation status updates
  - Retrospective discussions
  - Year 2 preliminary planning
