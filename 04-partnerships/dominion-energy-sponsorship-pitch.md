## 🎯 The Opportunity

**Dominion Energy has a unique opportunity to be the infrastructure sponsor that literally and metaphorically "powers" Richmond's first Civic Innovation Hackathon.**

This isn't just logo placement—this is brand integration at the core of what makes the event possible.

---

## ⚡ The Perfect Brand Alignment

### Dominion's Strategic Priorities
✅ **Sustainability & Climate Action** - Hackathon includes RVAgreen 2050 challenges  
✅ **Community Investment** - Supporting civic innovation in their headquarters city  
✅ **Innovation Leadership** - Positioned as enabler of technological progress  
✅ **Economic Development** - Supporting Richmond's tech ecosystem growth  
✅ **Literal Mission Alignment** - "Powering" has double meaning

### Why This Works Better Than Generic Sponsorship
Instead of: "Dominion is a sponsor"  
We say: **"This hackathon is powered by Dominion Energy"**

The metaphor is perfect:
- Just as Dominion powers Richmond's homes and businesses
- They're powering the innovation that will solve Richmond's challenges
- Physical infrastructure (power strips) = symbolic infrastructure (enabling solutions)

## 🔌 What Dominion Would Provide

### Physical Infrastructure
- **Power strips** for all hacking tables (100-150 tables × 1-2 strips each)
- **Extension cords** and power distribution equipment
- **Charging stations** for phones/devices (5-10 stations around venue)
- **Backup power** solutions if needed
- **Technical support** during event (if power issues arise)

### Brand Integration
- **"Powered by Dominion Energy"** branding throughout event
- Dominion logo on every power strip (physical brand presence)
- Signage: "Innovation Charging Station - Powered by Dominion"
- Opening remarks thanking Dominion for "powering our innovation"
- Social media: "600+ devices running thanks to @DominionEnergy"

### Budget Value
- **Equipment cost:** $500-1,000 (power strips, cords, charging stations)
- **Brand value:** Immeasurable (integrated into event narrative)
- **Tier recommendation:** Silver ($4,500-6,000) with in-kind infrastructure + cash

## 📝 Objection Handling

**"We've already allocated our community giving budget for the year"**
> "We understand. This could be considered for next fiscal year, or we could explore an in-kind infrastructure donation (power strips, charging stations) which might come from a different budget line. The brand value of 'Powering Innovation' would still be significant."

**"How is this different from other sponsorships we do?"**
> "This is unique because your brand is integrated into the fabric of the event, not just logos on a banner. Every participant interacts with Dominion-branded power strips at their table. The 'Powering Innovation' narrative is memorable and shareable in a way generic sponsorship isn't."

**"What if the projects don't succeed / get implemented?"**
> "That's why we've built an implementation pathway. The Mayor's Office is partnering with us, City departments are defining challenges, and we're requiring open-source licensing. We've done extensive research on what makes civic hackathons succeed. But even if implementation takes time, the brand association with innovation and community problem-solving is immediate."

**"We already sponsor tech events"**
> "This isn't just a tech event—it's a civic innovation partnership with the Mayor's Office. The solutions will address Richmond's real challenges: climate resilience, small business support, City services. This positions Dominion as a civic partner, not just a tech sponsor."

**"Can you send us more information?"**
> "Absolutely. I'll send our sponsorship prospectus, challenge descriptions, and partnership details. But I'd love to schedule a brief call to walk through why this opportunity is uniquely suited for Dominion. When works for you this week?"
