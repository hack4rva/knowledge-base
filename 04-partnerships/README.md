## 🎯 Strategic Focus: Mayor's Pillar 4 - A Thriving Economy

**Key Strategic Shift (November 12, 2025):**

The hackathon is now focused specifically on **Mayor Avula's Pillar 4: A Thriving Economy** rather than all 7 pillars. This provides:
- **Clear messaging:** Easier to explain value to partners
- **Authentic alignment:** Community stakeholders genuinely connect to economic development
- **Stronger value prop:** Economic development organizations see direct relevance
- **Better integration:** Connects to Richmond's economic ecosystem

**Partnership Philosophy:**

We want to connect Richmond's powerful organizations who are **funders and community connectors** - not just companies with money, but organizations authentically aligned with economic development and the Mayor's vision.

### Mission-Aligned Sponsored Award Model

**How it works:**
1. Approach community stakeholder organization
2. Explain Pillar 4: Thriving Economy focus
3. Ask: "How do you want to be part of this event?"
4. They sponsor an **award category they define** (aligned with their mission)
5. They determine award criteria and what it recognizes
6. Authentic connection to their organizational work (not just branding)
7. They present award at Sunday ceremony
8. Post-event: Winning teams connect to their resources

**Benefits:**
- Organizations feel genuine ownership
- Awards reflect Richmond's economic priorities
- Not transactional - true partnership
- Community stakeholders become event champions
- Solutions directly serve stakeholder missions

**Example:**
- **StartupVA sponsors:** "Best Entrepreneurial Solution" award ($1,500)
- **They define criteria:** Scalability, market validation, founder support needs
- **They present award:** StartupVA leader at Sunday ceremony
- **Post-event:** Winning team connects to StartupVA accelerator resources

## 💰 Sponsorship Tiers

### Diamond Tier ($10,000 - $15,000)
**Target: 1 sponsor (max 25% of budget)**

**Benefits:**
- Event title: "Presented by [Company]"
- Solo social media feature
- Extended keynote speaking opportunity (15 min)
- Dedicated incubation path for winning projects
- Prime logo placement (all materials)
- Exclusive VIP networking with Mayor and City leadership
- Guaranteed seat on judging panel
- Private room for interviews/meetings
- Access to all participant resumes
- 10 mentor volunteer spots

### Gold Tier ($7,500 - $9,000)
**Target: 2-3 sponsors**

**Benefits:**
- Large logo placement
- Branded challenge track/prize category
- Exclusive banner placement (venue)
- Large on-site interview space
- Tech talk slot (30 min)
- Resume book access
- Judging panel participation
- 5 mentor volunteer spots

### Silver Tier ($4,500 - $6,000)
**Target: 3-5 sponsors**

**Benefits:**
- Medium logo placement
- Tech talk slot (15 min)
- Small on-site interview space
- Resume book access
- Swag distribution
- 3 mentor volunteer spots

### Bronze Tier ($2,000 - $3,000)
**Target: 5-8 sponsors**

**Benefits:**
- Basic logo placement
- Swag distribution
- Resume book access
- Company profile in participant materials
- 1 mentor volunteer spot

### The Technical (Cloud/Hosting/Tooling)
**Purpose:** Provide consistent technical infrastructure and tooling to all teams

**Event Format Context**: This is an **in-person hackathon** at CoStar's Richmond office. All participants attend in person for check-in, hacking, and judging (Friday 12pm-10pm). Cloud/hosting tools are for:
- Development during the in-person event weekend
- Post-event hosting and sustainability
- Reducing infrastructure costs during pilot implementations

**Strategic Importance:**
- Eliminates hosting cost barriers to implementation
- Reduces "works on my machine" problems during in-person collaboration
- Levels playing field for all participants working on-site
- Enables post-event sustainability (credits extending 6-12 months)
- Reduces micro-grant burden for infrastructure costs
- Professional tools attract serious participants

**Partnership Categories:**

#### Cloud Infrastructure Providers (Choose 1-2 Primary)

**AWS (Amazon Web Services)**
- **Standard Offering**: $100 credits per participant through AWS Activate
- **Approval Timeline**: 7-10 business days
- **Special Features**: AWS Cloud9 for collaborative development, pre-configured environments
- **Support**: Potential Solutions Architect availability during event
- **Application**: AWS Activate Credits program, requires valid event proof
- **Best For**: Teams needing full cloud infrastructure (compute, storage, databases)
- **Research Status**: ✅ Comprehensive research completed (see research.md)
- **Contact**: AWS Activate Credits team
- **Priority**: HIGH - Most comprehensive hackathon support program

**Microsoft Azure**
- **Standard Offering**: 
  - $200 automatic for new accounts (30-day validity)
  - $1,000 per team via Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub (first 500 teams)
- **Approval Timeline**: Immediate for $200, request-based for $1,000
- **Special Features**: Azure Lab Services for rapid VM provisioning, GitHub Copilot integration
- **Credit Duration**: 90-day activation window, 2-year usage once activated
- **Best For**: Teams building with Microsoft stack, AI/ML with Azure OpenAI
- **Research Status**: ✅ Comprehensive research completed (see research.md)
- **Contact**: Microsoft for Startups team
- **Priority**: HIGH - Fastest credit access, generous amounts

**Google Cloud Platform**
- **Standard Offering**: $100 credits through hackathon request form
- **Approval Timeline**: 48 business hours
- **Special Features**: Cloud Shell, pre-configured templates, strong quickstart libraries
- **Best For**: Teams needing fast setup with templates
- **Research Status**: ✅ Comprehensive research completed (see research.md)
- **Contact**: Google Cloud for Startups team
- **Priority**: MEDIUM-HIGH - Fast approval, good templates
