## 🎯 Research Philosophy: Strategic Partnerships, Not Transactional Sponsorships

### What We're Looking For
✅ **Strategic fit** - Company mission aligns with civic innovation  
✅ **Challenge alignment** - Sponsor cares about specific challenges we're tackling  
✅ **Long-term potential** - Could become multi-year partner  
✅ **Network value** - Opens doors beyond just money  
✅ **Brand enhancement** - Our event enhances their brand, they enhance ours

### What We're NOT Looking For
❌ Generic logo-on-banner sponsorships  
❌ Companies that don't care about Richmond  
❌ One-time check writers with no engagement  
❌ Misalignment with civic mission  
❌ Sponsors who want control without partnership

---

## 📊 Sponsor Research Framework

### For Each Prospect Company, Research:

**1. Basic Information**
- Company name and industry
- Richmond presence (HQ, major office, satellite)
- Employee count in Richmond
- Annual revenue / market position

**2. Community Investment Profile**
- Do they have CSR / community giving program?
- What causes do they support? (education, environment, economic development, tech, etc.)
- What Richmond organizations do they already support?
- Annual giving budget (if public)
- Recent sponsorships or partnerships

**3. Decision-Maker Intelligence**
- Who approves sponsorships? (Title: Director of Community Relations, CSR Manager, etc.)
- Contact information (LinkedIn, email, phone)
- Budget cycle timing (fiscal year, planning windows)
- Typical sponsorship range (based on other events)

**4. Strategic Fit Analysis**
- Challenge alignment score (which MAP pillars resonate?)
- Cultural fit (progressive, innovation-focused, civic-minded?)
- Brand alignment (does our event enhance their brand?)
- Competitive positioning (are competitors sponsoring?)

**5. Connection Mapping**
- Does anyone on our team know someone at this company?
- Who introduced? (warm intro vs. cold outreach)
- Relationship strength (strong, medium, weak)
- Path to decision-maker

**6. Sponsorship History**
- Have they sponsored RVA Tech events?
- VCU events? Other local tech/civic events?
- Typical tier/amount?
- What got them excited?

## 💡 Strategic Fit Scoring

### Score Each Prospect (1-5 scale):

**Mission Alignment (1-5):**
- Does company mission align with civic innovation?
- Do they care about Richmond's future?

**Challenge Fit (1-5):**
- Do our challenges align with their CSR priorities?
- Can we tell a compelling story about their support?

**Brand Value (1-5):**
- Does their brand enhance our credibility?
- Does our event enhance their brand?

**Budget Capacity (1-5):**
- Can they afford our tiers ($2K-15K)?
- History of sponsorships at this level?

**Engagement Potential (1-5):**
- Will they want to be involved beyond money?
- Employee volunteers? Mentors? Long-term partnership?

**Total Score: /25**
- 20-25: Priority tier (pursue immediately)
- 15-19: Strong prospect (pursue soon)
- 10-14: Good prospect (pursue if time/capacity)
- <10: Reconsider fit

## 🎯 Sponsor Outreach Sequence

### After Research Complete:

**Tier 1: Warm Intros (Highest Success Rate)**
1. Identify connection path
2. Request intro from mutual contact
3. Send personalized pitch via intro
4. Follow up within 48 hours
5. Schedule meeting within 1 week

**Tier 2: Direct Outreach (Lower Success, Still Viable)**
1. Personalized email to decision-maker
2. Reference research ("I saw you sponsor...")
3. Lead with alignment story
4. Follow up with call in 3 days
5. Send prospectus if interest

**Tier 3: Event-Based Outreach (Via Happy Hour)**
1. Invite to leadership happy hour
2. Soft pitch during event
3. Follow up next day with formal ask
4. Leverage Mayor's endorsement from event
