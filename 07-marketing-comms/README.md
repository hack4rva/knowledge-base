## 🎯 Target Audiences

### Students (VCU, UR, VUU)
**Messaging Focus:**
- Portfolio-building opportunity
- Real-world civic implementation
- Networking with City policymakers and CoStar professionals
- Unique Friday format accommodates class schedules

**Channels:**
- University email lists
- Professor announcements
- Student organization partnerships
- Campus poster campaign with QR codes

**Barrier Mitigation:**
- Work with professors for excused absences/extra credit
- Emphasize half-day commitment (not full weekend)

### Working Professionals (RVA Tech Community)
**Messaging Focus:**
- Civic fulfillment
- Immediate, tangible impact on Richmond
- Minimal work disruption (12 PM start = half-day PTO)
- High-value networking

**Channels:**
- RVA Tech meetups and user groups
- LinkedIn targeting
- Corporate email outreach
- Professional association partnerships

### City Employees
**Messaging Focus:**
- Sanctioned professional development
- Solve problems in your own department
- Mayor's Office supported
- During regular work hours

**Channels:**
- Internal city communications
- Department liaisons
- Mayor's Office endorsement

### Diverse Communities & Underrepresented Groups
**Messaging Focus:**
- "Every citizen has something to add"
- Collaborative, not competitive
- Non-technical roles welcome
- Learning and skill-building

**Channels:**
- Women Who Code RVA
- Code for VA
- Community organizations
- Scholarship/bursary program

## 🎯 Messaging Framework

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
> "Hack for RVA is Richmond's premier civic innovation hackathon, bringing together technologists, designers, and changemakers for one day to solve real problems for our city. Co-hosted by the Mayor's Office and CoStar Group, we're building prototypes that will actually get implemented—turning civic challenges into civic solutions."

### Key Messages
1. **City-Endorsed:** Official Mayor's Office support ensures real impact
2. **Implementation-Focused:** Not just ideas—actual deployed solutions
3. **Inclusive:** Welcoming all skill levels and backgrounds
4. **Community-Driven:** By Richmonders, for Richmond
5. **10-Year Vision:** Building a sustainable civic innovation ritual
