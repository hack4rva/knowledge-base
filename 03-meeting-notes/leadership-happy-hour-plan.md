## 🎯 Event Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. **Secure Mayor's visible support** - Get commitment for March 6-8 participation
2. **Recruit influential champions** - People who can open doors and advocate
3. **Build advisory network** - Form informal "kitchen cabinet" for the event
4. **Generate early buzz** - Create FOMO and momentum before public launch
5. **Identify challenge sponsors** - Connect influencers to specific civic challenges
6. **Validate format and vision** - Get feedback from experienced civic leaders

### Success Metrics
- Mayor commits to attending event + speaking at Sunday ceremony
- 3-5 influential leaders agree to be "Advisory Committee" or champions
- 2-3 potential sponsors identified for follow-up
- Social proof for fundraising ("Mayor was at planning meeting")
- Actionable feedback on format and challenges

## 💬 Key Talking Points for Ford

### The Elevator Pitch (60 seconds)
> "Richmond has an incredible tech workforce - CoStar, Capital One, CarMax, VCU students - but we've never channeled that talent toward solving our city's problems. Mayor Avula's Mayoral Action Plan identifies critical challenges: modernizing City Hall services, achieving RVAgreen 2050 goals, helping small businesses thrive. We're creating a weekend where Richmond's technologists build solutions the City will actually implement. CoStar is hosting, the Mayor's Office is partnering, and we need Richmond's leaders to make this the civic innovation ritual our city deserves."

### Why This Matters (The Stakes)
- **For the City:** Cost-efficient way to prototype solutions before expensive RFPs
- **For Tech Community:** Meaning and purpose, not just building shareholder value
- **For Sponsors:** Authentic civic engagement, talent pipeline, brand association with innovation
- **For Richmond:** Shows we can solve our own problems, builds civic pride

### The Ask (Be Specific)
Depending on who you're talking to:

**To Potential Sponsors:**
> "We're looking for sponsors at the [$tier] level. Your support would [specific benefit]. Can we set up a meeting next week to discuss?"

**To Influencers/Connectors:**
> "We'd love your advice as we shape this. Would you join an informal advisory committee? Just 2-3 conversations over the next few months."

**To City Council:**
> "This directly supports the Mayor's Action Plan. We'd love your department's input on challenges to tackle. Can you connect us with [specific person]?"

**To Corporate Leaders:**
> "Your engineers could mentor, your recruiting team could scout talent, your brand could be associated with solving real Richmond problems. Let's talk about partnership."

### Handling Questions

**"Why Friday-Sunday vs. just Saturday?"**
> "Accessibility. Working professionals can come Friday, students and parents can come Saturday, everyone can celebrate together Sunday. We want ALL of Richmond, not just those who can sacrifice a full weekend."

**"Will projects actually get implemented?"**
> "That's the whole point. We're pre-vetting challenges with City departments, identifying Implementation Champions, and setting up a 6-month mentorship pathway with micro-grants. This isn't just ideas - it's procurement."

**"How is this different from other hackathons?"**
> "Three ways: First, it's civic-focused with City partnership. Second, challenges are scoped for actual implementation, not just demos. Third, it's designed to become a 10-year ritual, not a one-off."

**"What's the budget? / How much do you need?"**
> "Total budget is about $46K. CoStar's donating $10K in venue and facilities. We're raising the rest through tiered sponsorships. We have [X] committed so far and are in conversations with [Y]."

**"How do we ensure diversity?"**
> "The format itself helps - multiple days accommodates different schedules. We're partnering with Women Who Code RVA, targeting VCU Women in CS, and actively recruiting through community organizations. Our target is 40% female/non-binary participation."
