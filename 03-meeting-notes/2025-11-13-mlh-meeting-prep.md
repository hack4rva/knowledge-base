## 📖 The Story (3-Minute Pitch)

### Opening Hook

Richmond is building something different. Not just a hackathon—a 10-year civic innovation ritual that connects the Mayor's Office, Fortune 500 corporate partners, and 150-200 builders to solve real economic development challenges.

**March 6-8, 2026. CoStar Headquarters. Mayor Danny Avula at kickoff and awards.**

### The Unique Angle

**This isn't a typical student hackathon.** It's a public-private partnership designed to build solutions the City will actually implement.

**Mayor's Pillar 4: A Thriving Economy** - we're laser-focused on one of the Mayor's seven strategic priorities. Economic development. Business permitting. Entrepreneurship support. Challenges that, if solved, become City services.

**The implementation pathway matters:** Winning solutions don't just get prizes. They get:
- $1,000-$5,000 micro-grants
- 6 months of City support
- Post-event incubation with mentorship
- A real shot at becoming City infrastructure
