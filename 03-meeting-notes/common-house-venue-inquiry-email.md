## Final Notes

### Why December vs. November?
Based on your note about "early December," this assumes:
- November is too soon (only 2-3 weeks out)
- Thanksgiving week is impossible
- Early December (first 2 weeks) gives enough lead time before holidays
- Still leaves January-February for intensive planning before March event

### Why Tuesday/Wednesday?
- Best attendance for professional events
- Thursday is second choice (some people start weekend early)
- Monday and Friday are worst for evening events
- Weekend not appropriate for this type of gathering

### Budget Reality Check
$2,000-$2,500 for 40-50 people at Common House might be tight depending on their pricing model. Be prepared to:
- Negotiate
- Adjust menu (fewer premium items)
- Bring in outside catering if allowed
- Scale back to 40 guaranteed vs. 50
