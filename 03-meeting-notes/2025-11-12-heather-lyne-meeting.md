## Partnership Model Evolution

### New Approach: Mission-Aligned Sponsored Awards

**Old thinking:** Ask organizations for general sponsorship money  
**New thinking:** Ask organizations to sponsor an award category **they define**

**How it works:**
1. Approach community stakeholder organization
2. Explain Pillar 4: Thriving Economy focus
3. Ask: "How do you want to be part of this event?"
4. They sponsor an award aligned with **their organizational mission**
5. They define what the award recognizes
6. Authentic connection to their work (not just branding)

**Benefits:**
- Organizations feel genuine ownership
- Awards reflect Richmond's economic priorities
- Not just transactional - true partnership
- Community stakeholders become event champions

**Example:**
- **StartupVA sponsors:** "Best Entrepreneurial Solution" award ($1,500)
- **They define criteria:** Scalability, market validation, founder support needs
- **They present award:** StartupVA leader at Sunday ceremony
- **Post-event:** Winning team connects to StartupVA resources
