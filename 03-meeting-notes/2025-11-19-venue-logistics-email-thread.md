## Logistical Considerations

### Capacity Management
- **Target:** 30-40 people (Ford's original estimate)
- **Max:** 50 people (venue limit)
- **Strategy:** Curate guest list carefully, quality over quantity
- **Challenge:** Cannot control member access to space

### Same Night as Oyster Roast
- **Opportunity:** Creates convergence of Richmond civic/political leaders
- **Challenge:** Common House may have competing traffic/noise
- **Strategy:** Frame as feature, not bug - "everyone's in the building"

### Member Access Issue
- Social Hall will not be closed to Common House members
- Members may be in space during event
- **Mitigation:** Use this as networking opportunity, not concern
- **Framing:** "Open, welcoming environment" vs "exclusive closed event"

### Contract & Payment
- Crystal Harvey signing contract on behalf of organizers
- 50% deposit required to secure space
- Balance + bar tab due after event
- **Action:** Crystal to sign and submit deposit ASAP
