# Participant Experience

Registration, onboarding, mentorship, diversity & inclusion, and support systems for hackathon participants.

---

## 🎯 Core Principles

### Zero Barrier to Entry
- **No registration fee** (adheres to MLH community values)
- **No experience required** - welcoming beginners
- **No special equipment needed** - just bring a laptop

### Inclusive by Design
- Deemphasize competition, emphasize learning
- Welcoming to all genders, races, skill levels
- Non-technical roles explicitly welcomed
- Combat imposter syndrome through messaging and structure

### Community-First
- "Every citizen has something to add"
- Collaborative over competitive
- Civic mission over technical prowess

## 📝 Registration Strategy

### Registration Platform Options
- **Eventbrite** - Simple, well-known
- **Devpost** - Hackathon-specific, project submission built-in
- **Custom form** - More control, more work
- **Recommendation:** Devpost for hackathon legitimacy

### Information to Collect
**Required:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Emergency contact
- Dietary restrictions
- Code of conduct agreement

**Optional but Recommended:**
- School/employer
- Skill level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
- Skills (Coding, Design, Data, Policy, Other)
- Interests and challenge preferences
- T-shirt size
- Team preferences (have team vs. need team)

### Team Formation

**Option 1: Pre-Formed Teams**
- Teams of 3-6 sign up together
- Team name and member list

**Option 2: Individual Matching**
- Individuals sign up solo
- Indicate preferences and skill sets
- Matched with complementary skills
- Team formation session at event (1:15 PM - 2:00 PM)

**Hybrid Approach (Recommended):**
- Allow both pre-formed and individual signups
- Reserve 2 hours at event start for team formation
- Provide "team canvas" activity for new teams

## 🎨 Diversity & Inclusion Strategy

### Current Hackathon Diversity Problem
- Typical gender ratio: **15% female, 85% male**
- Women and non-binary people of color: **2-4% at MLH events**
- Severe imposter syndrome in underrepresented groups

### Our Target
- **40% female/non-binary participation** (KPI)
- Racial diversity reflecting Richmond demographics
- Mix of students, professionals, and community members
- Age diversity (high schoolers to seniors)

### "Clever Marketing" Approach

**Avoid:**
- Overly competitive language
- "Elite" or "expert" framing
- Intimidating technical jargon
- Prize-focused messaging

**Emphasize:**
- Cross-collaboration
- Learning opportunities
- Skill-building
- Civic impact
- Community and connection

### Judging Criteria Alignment
- **Not just technical execution**
- Civic impact and feasibility weighted heavily
- User validation and collaboration scored
- Low-fidelity prototypes welcomed
- Non-technical contributions valued

## 💻 Technical Resources & Tooling

### Overview
All participants receive access to professional development tools and hosting platforms, eliminating cost barriers and ensuring consistent infrastructure across all teams.

**Note**: This is an **in-person event** at CoStar's Richmond office. All participants must attend in person for check-in and judging. Cloud tools and platforms are for development purposes during the event (and post-event hosting), not to enable remote participation.

### Cloud Infrastructure (Choose Your Stack)

**AWS Credits**
- **What You Get**: $100 in AWS credits per participant
- **Good For**: Full cloud infrastructure (compute, storage, databases)
- **How to Access**: Pre-event signup link, 7-10 day approval process
- **Special Feature**: AWS Cloud9 collaborative coding environment
- **Duration**: 12 months from issuance

**Microsoft Azure Credits**
- **What You Get**: $200 automatic + up to $1,000 per team
- **Good For**: Microsoft stack, AI/ML with Azure OpenAI
- **How to Access**: Immediate signup, team grants via application
- **Duration**: 30 days for $200, 2 years for team grants

**Google Cloud Platform Credits**
- **What You Get**: $100 in GCP credits
- **Good For**: Quick setup with templates and quickstarts
- **How to Access**: Pre-event signup, 48-hour approval
- **Duration**: 12 months from issuance

### Hosting & Deployment Platforms

**Vercel (Recommended for Web Apps)**
- **What You Get**: Pro account access during event + 6 months for winners
- **Good For**: Web applications, Next.js, React, Vue
- **Why Use**: Easiest deployment, automatic HTTPS, instant preview URLs
- **Perfect For**: Civic information sites, dashboards, web tools

**Railway (Recommended for Full-Stack Apps)**
- **What You Get**: $10-20/month credits for 6 months
- **Good For**: Full-stack apps with PostgreSQL, Redis databases
- **Why Use**: Simple infrastructure, includes database hosting
- **Perfect For**: Civic apps needing data persistence

**Render**
- **What You Get**: Credits or upgraded account
- **Good For**: Alternative to Railway, similar features
- **Perfect For**: Full-stack applications, APIs

**Netlify**
- **What You Get**: Team account access
- **Good For**: Static sites, JAMstack applications
- **Perfect For**: Simple civic information sites

### No-Code/Low-Code Tools (Beginner-Friendly!)

**Lovable.dev** 🌟
- **What You Get**: Premium access during event + 6 months for winners
- **Good For**: Building professional web apps without coding
- **Why Use**: AI-powered, dramatically faster prototyping
- **Perfect For**: Non-technical team members, rapid UI development, civic interfaces
- **Accessibility**: No coding experience required!

**Replit**
- **What You Get**: Team/Hacker plan during event
- **Good For**: Browser-based collaborative coding
- **Why Use**: No local setup needed, multiple languages
- **Perfect For**: Quick prototyping, team collaboration

**Glide**
- **What You Get**: Team account access
- **Good For**: No-code apps from spreadsheets
- **Perfect For**: Data-driven civic dashboards and mobile apps

### Developer Tools

**GitHub + Copilot**
- **What You Get**: GitHub Pro + Copilot AI assistance
- **Good For**: Version control (essential!) + AI coding help
- **Why Use**: Industry standard, team collaboration
- **Required**: Yes - all teams should use version control

**Cursor IDE** (if partnership secured)
- **What You Get**: Pro license during event
- **Good For**: AI-powered coding assistance
- **Perfect For**: Experienced developers wanting AI acceleration

### API Services & Integration

**Twilio**
- **What You Get**: $50-100 in API credits
- **Good For**: SMS, voice, video features
- **Use Cases**: Civic alerts, communication tools, hotlines

**Stripe**
- **What You Get**: Test mode access + documentation
- **Good For**: Payment processing
- **Use Cases**: Donation features, fee payment tools

### Technical Onboarding Process

**3 Weeks Before Event**
- [ ] Email with links to all technical partnerships
- [ ] Instructions for creating accounts and applying for credits
- [ ] FAQ document for common setup issues
- [ ] Slack/Discord channel for technical questions

**1 Week Before Event**
- [ ] Reminder to complete account setup
- [ ] Verification that credits have been approved
- [ ] Troubleshooting support for setup issues

**Day 1: Technical Setup Workshop (Hour 2)**
- **10:00-11:00 AM**: Hands-on setup session
- Topics covered:
  - Choosing the right platform for your project
  - Creating first deployment (demo)
  - Setting up team collaboration (GitHub, Cloud9, or Replit)
  - Troubleshooting access issues
- **Support**: Technical mentors available for individual help

**During Event**
- "Ask a Tech Expert" support channel
- Documentation on event website
- Pre-configured templates and quickstarts
- Roving technical support volunteers

### Best Practices for Participants

**Choose Wisely**
- Pick tools that match your team's skill level
- Don't try to learn too many new tools during the event
- Use no-code tools if you're non-technical - they're powerful!

**Get Help Early**
- Setup issues compound quickly
- Ask mentors during Day 1 setup workshop
- Don't waste Saturday debugging access problems

**Think About Sustainability**
- Choose platforms with post-event credit extensions
- Winning teams get 6-month hosting support
- Consider what the City can maintain long-term

### Post-Event Technical Support

**All Teams**
- Access to deployment documentation and guides
- Community Slack for ongoing questions
- Credits remain active for 6-12 months (platform dependent)

**Winning Teams / Incubation Program**
- Extended credits and platform access (6 months minimum)
- Dedicated technical mentor from CoStar
- Priority support for deployment issues
- Hosting covered during pilot phase

**Value to Participants**
- **Estimated value**: $100-1,500 per team in free credits and tools
- **Career benefit**: Experience with professional development platforms
- **Portfolio building**: Live deployed projects for resumes
- **No cost barrier**: Free hosting removes implementation obstacle
