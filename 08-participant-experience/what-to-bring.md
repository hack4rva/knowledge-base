# What to Bring to Hack for RVA

**Quick reference checklist for participants**

---

## ✅ MUST BRING (Essential)

### 💻 Technology
- [ ] **Laptop** (fully charged)
- [ ] **Laptop charger** (non-negotiable!)
- [ ] **Phone** + charger

### 📋 Documents
- [ ] **Government ID or Student ID** (required for venue check-in)
- [ ] **Confirmation email** (digital or printed)
- [ ] **Signed waiver** (if not completed online)

---

## 💡 SHOULD BRING (Highly Recommended)

### Tech Gear
- [ ] **Power strip** (share with your team - very helpful!)
- [ ] **Mouse** (if you prefer external)
- [ ] **Headphones** (for focus time)
- [ ] **Phone charging cable**
- [ ] **USB drives** (backup your work!)

### Comfort
- [ ] **Water bottle** (refillable - stay hydrated!)
- [ ] **Layers** (sweater/jacket - room temps vary in office buildings)
- [ ] **Comfortable clothes** (you'll be sitting a lot)
- [ ] **Comfortable shoes** (for walking around venue)

### Work Tools
- [ ] **Notebook and pen** (brainstorming, notes, sketches)
- [ ] **Business cards** (if you want to network)
- [ ] **Resume** (if interested in sponsor conversations)

---

## 🎒 NICE TO HAVE (Optional)

### Extra Tech
- [ ] External monitor (if you have portable one)
- [ ] Keyboard (if you prefer external)
- [ ] Webcam (for better Discord calls)
- [ ] Drawing tablet (for designers)
- [ ] Hardware/sensors (if building physical prototype - check with organizers first!)

### Personal Items
- [ ] Snacks you love (we provide food, but personal favorites are nice)
- [ ] Coffee mug (if you're particular about your coffee)
- [ ] Eye drops or glasses (screen time relief)
- [ ] Hand sanitizer
- [ ] Pain reliever (headache, back pain from sitting)
- [ ] Deodorant (be kind to your teammates!)
- [ ] Phone battery pack
- [ ] Toothbrush/toothpaste (for freshening up)

### Comfort Items (if doing both days)
- [ ] Pillow or travel pillow
- [ ] Blanket or hoodie
- [ ] Change of clothes
- [ ] Toiletries

### Work Materials
- [ ] Whiteboard markers (if using venue whiteboards)
- [ ] Post-it notes (brainstorming)
- [ ] Sketchbook (for designers)
- [ ] Reference books (if you use them)

---

## ❌ DO NOT BRING

- ❌ **Weapons** of any kind
- ❌ **Alcohol** or illegal drugs (alcohol-free event)
- ❌ **Pets** (except service animals - notify organizers in advance)
- ❌ **Expensive valuables** you can't afford to lose
- ❌ **Drones or loud equipment** (without prior organizer approval)
- ❌ **Fireworks, explosives, anything dangerous**
- ❌ **Soldering irons or heat tools** (fire safety - check with organizers if needed)

**Note:** CoStar security may check bags. Please respect venue rules.

## 🎯 Mental Preparation

### Set Realistic Expectations

**You will:**
- ✅ Learn something new
- ✅ Meet interesting people
- ✅ Build something (even if not "perfect")
- ✅ Have fun
- ✅ Contribute to Richmond

**You might:**
- 💭 Feel overwhelmed at times (normal!)
- 💭 Get stuck on technical problems (ask for help!)
- 💭 Change your project idea (totally okay!)
- 💭 Not finish everything you planned (scope happens!)

**You probably won't:**
- 🤷 Build the perfect solution (and that's okay!)
- 🤷 Win a prize (most teams don't, still valuable!)
- 🤷 Know everything (nobody does!)

### Mindset Tips

**Do:**
- Come with curiosity and openness
- Be willing to learn and try new things
- Collaborate and help others
- Ask questions without shame
- Embrace imperfection
- Focus on impact over perfection

**Don't:**
- Compare yourself to others
- Stress about "being good enough"
- Work alone if you're stuck
- Skip meals and sleep
- Give up when it gets hard
- Forget why you're here (to help Richmond!)
