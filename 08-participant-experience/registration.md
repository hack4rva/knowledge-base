## 📝 Registration Platform Selection

**Why Devpost:**
- ✅ Industry standard for hackathons

- ✅ Familiar to hackathon participants
- ✅ Good sponsor showcase features
- ✅ Analytics and reporting tools

**Alternative Options:**
- Eventbrite (simpler but no project submission)
- Google Forms + Airtable (more manual work)
- Custom solution (unnecessary complexity)

## 👥 Team Formation Options

### Option 1: Pre-Formed Teams (Recommended)

**Team Size:** 3-6 members

**Registration Process:**
1. Designate one person as team captain
2. Captain registers and creates team
3. Captain invites team members by email
4. All members complete individual registration
5. All members linked to team in system

**Team Requirements:**
- All members must register individually
- All must accept all terms/policies
- Team name must be appropriate (subject to organizer approval)
- At least 1 member must attend each day team is participating

### Option 2: Individual Registration (Team Matching)

**Registration Process:**
1. Register as individual
2. Indicate skills and interests
3. System suggests potential teammates OR
4. Attend team formation session at event

**Team Formation Session:**
- **Friday:** 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM
- **Saturday:** 9:30 AM - 10:30 AM
- Facilitated team matching activity
- "Team Canvas" icebreaker exercise
- Organized by skill/interest complementarity

### Option 3: Hybrid (Flexible)

- Some pre-formed team members + recruit additional members at event
- Friday team merges with Saturday newcomers
- Open to changing team composition during event

**Recommended:** Form teams in advance for smoother experience, but team matching available.

### Capacity Management

**Maximum Attendance:**

**Registration Strategy:**

### Waitlist Process

If event reaches capacity:
1. Waitlist automatically created
2. Confirmation email if spot opens
3. 48-hour window to confirm
4. Priority: underrepresented groups, complete teams

### Day-Of Team Formation

**Friday Session (11:00 AM - 12:30 PM):**
- Welcome and icebreaker
- Skills showcase ("I can... I need...")
- Facilitated team matching
- Team Canvas activity (goals, skills, communication)
- Team naming and registration

**Saturday Session (9:30 AM - 10:30 AM):**
- Similar process for Saturday arrivals
- Option to join Friday teams
- New team formation for Saturday-only folks

**Team Formation Tips:**
- Aim for 4-5 people (optimal collaboration)
- Balance technical and non-technical skills
- Include at least one experienced hacker per team
- Consider time commitment compatibility
- Establish communication norms early
