**Your Guide to Richmond's First Civic Hackathon**

Welcome! This guide will help you know what to expect at Hack for RVA on March 6-8, 2026.

## 🎯 What IS a Hackathon?

A hackathon is a weekend-long innovation marathon where people collaborate to build solutions to real problems.

**"Hacking" means:**
- Creative problem-solving (not breaking into computers!)
- Rapid prototyping and building
- Learning new skills
- Working with a team
- Building something meaningful

**This is a CIVIC hackathon, which means:**
- We're solving real Richmond problems
- Working with the Mayor's Office
- Building solutions that can actually be implemented
- Focused on community benefit, not profit

## 👥 What About Teams?

### Team Sizes
- **Recommended:** 4-5 people
- **Minimum:** 1 person (solo allowed!)
- **Maximum:** 6 people

### Team Roles

**A great team has:**
- **Coder(s):** Build the technical solution (1-3 people)
- **Designer:** Create user interface and experience (1 person)
- **Data Person:** Work with datasets and analysis (1 person)
- **Presenter:** Communicate the solution clearly (1 person)
- **Community Expert:** Ensure solution serves real needs (1 person)

**You don't need all these roles!** Mix and match based on your team.

### Don't Have a Team?

**No problem!** We'll help you find teammates:

**Option 1: Pre-Event Matching**
- We'll suggest teammates based on your skills/interests
- Introduce yourselves in Discord
- Optional pre-event meetup (virtual or coffee)

**Option 2: Day-Of Team Formation**
- Friday 11:00 AM or Saturday 9:30 AM
- Fun icebreaker activities
- Skill showcase ("I can... I need...")
- Form teams on the spot

**Don't stress!** Many successful hackathon projects come from teams that meet at the event.

## 🎨 First Time at a Hackathon?

### You're Not Alone!

**Common worries:**
- "I'm not good enough" → **False!** All skill levels welcome
- "I don't know how to code" → **That's okay!** Many valuable roles don't require coding
- "I won't know anyone" → **Perfect!** Great way to meet people
- "I'll slow my team down" → **Nope!** Everyone contributes differently

### What Beginners Should Know

**It's okay to:**
- Ask "basic" questions (mentors love helping!)
- Use no-code tools (Lovable.dev, Glide, templates)
- Focus on design, research, or presentation
- Learn as you go
- Make mistakes
- Take breaks when you need them

**It's NOT okay to:**
- Put yourself down
- Skip meals and sleep
- Work alone if you're stuck (ask for help!)
- Quit because something's hard (mentors are here!)

### Resources for Beginners

**Pre-Event:**
- Intro to GitHub tutorial
- Intro to Python/JavaScript basics
- No-code tool tutorials (Lovable, Glide)
- Design thinking basics

**At Event:**
- "Ask a Beginner Question" help desk
- Intro workshops Friday afternoon
- Mentors specifically for beginners
- Peer buddies (match experienced with new)

## 🤝 What Are Mentors?

### Mentors Are Here to Help

**Mentors are:**
- Technical experts (engineers, designers, data scientists)
- Domain experts (City employees, policy professionals)
- Community organizers who know Richmond
- There to answer questions and give feedback

**Mentors can:**
- Help you debug code
- Suggest technical approaches
- Explain City data or processes
- Give UX feedback
- Help scope your project
- Connect you to resources

**Mentors cannot:**
- Do the work for you
- Join your team (they're helping everyone)
- Make decisions for you
- Guarantee your project will win

### How to Work with Mentors

**Ask questions like:**
- "What's the best way to approach this problem?"
- "Can you help me debug this error?"
- "Does this design make sense for users?"
- "Is this project scope realistic?"

**Be respectful:**
- Mentors are volunteering their time
- Come prepared with specific questions
- Take notes on their advice
- Say thank you!

## 🏆 Judging & Prizes

### What Judges Look For

**It's NOT just about code!**

**Judging Criteria:**
1. **Civic Impact** (30%) - Does this solve a real Richmond problem?
2. **Feasibility** (25%) - Can the City actually implement this?
3. **User Validation** (20%) - Did you talk to potential users?
4. **Execution** (15%) - Does it work? Is it polished?
5. **Collaboration** (10%) - Did diverse skills contribute?

**See:** [judging-criteria.md](../06-challenge-design/judging-criteria.md) [TBD] for full rubric

**Total:** $12,500+ in prizes

**Prize Distribution:**

## 🎤 Demo Day (Sunday)

### What's a Demo?

**A demo is a 3-minute presentation** of what you built.

**You'll show:**
- The problem you solved
- Who it helps
- How it works (live demo preferred!)
- Why the City should implement it

### Demo Format

**3 minutes total:**
- 2 minutes: Presentation
- 1 minute: Quick Q&A from judges

**Tips for Great Demos:**
- Practice beforehand!
- Show, don't just tell
- Make it visual
- Focus on impact, not technical details
- Speak clearly and enthusiastically
- Have backup plan if tech fails

### Who Presents?

- Anyone on your team can present
- Can have multiple people present together
- Choose best presenter(s)
- Practice together

## ❓ FAQs

### General

**Q: Is this really free?**  
A: Yes! No registration fee. Food provided. All tools and resources free.

**Q: What if I've never been to a hackathon?**  
A: Perfect! We're designed for first-timers. Mentors will help you.

**Q: Do I need to know how to code?**  
A: Nope! We need designers, researchers, policy experts, community leaders. Use no-code tools if you want!

**Q: Can I work on something I've already started?**  
A: You can build on existing ideas, but majority of work must happen during the event.

### Logistics

**Q: Can I leave and come back?**  
A: Yes! Just keep your badge for re-entry. Check out with security.

**Q: Where do I park?**  
A: [TBD - CoStar parking details]

**Q: What if I'm running late?**  
A: Registration desk open until 12 PM Friday, 11 AM Saturday. You'll miss opening but can catch up.

**Q: Can I bring my own food?**  
A: Yes, especially if you have severe allergies. But we're providing great food!

### Teams & Projects

**Q: What if I don't have a team?**  
A: We'll match you! Team formation sessions Friday morning and Saturday morning.

**Q: Can I change teams after forming?**  
A: Generally yes, check with organizers.

**Q: What if my team falls apart?**  
A: Talk to organizers. We'll help you join another team or continue solo.

**Q: Do I have to build something from scratch?**  
A: Mostly yes. You can use existing libraries, templates, APIs, but the solution should be new.

### Technical

**Q: What if the WiFi fails?**  
A: We have backup plans! Also can hotspot if needed.

**Q: What if I can't get cloud credits approved?**  
A: Technical mentors will help. Also have backup accounts available.

**Q: Can I use my own tools/platforms?**  
A: Yes! Provided tools are optional (but helpful).

**Q: What if my laptop breaks?**  
A: We may have loaner laptops [TBD - verify with CoStar]. Also team members can share.

### Prizes

**Q: Do I have to win to participate?**  
A: NO! Most teams don't win prizes. The experience, learning, and impact matter most.

**Q: What if I win but can't make Sunday?**  
A: You must attend Sunday to receive prizes. No exceptions.

**Q: How are team prizes split?**  
A: Teams decide! Work this out among yourselves.
