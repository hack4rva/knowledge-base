## 📋 Archive Policy

### When to Archive

Documents should be moved to archive when:
- A newer version supersedes them
- The information is no longer current
- The approach or strategy has changed
- The document served a specific time-limited purpose

### What to Keep Active

Keep documents active if:
- Still referenced regularly
- Contains current, actionable information
- Part of ongoing processes
- Required for compliance or legal reasons
