## 💡 Best Practices

### From Strategic Research

**Venue & Logistics:**
- High-density WiFi is NON-NEGOTIABLE - standard venue WiFi always fails
- F&B claim rates: 90% Day 1 lunch, drops to 30% late event
- Power: 1 strip per table minimum
- 12 PM - 10 PM format optimal for Friday event (half-day PTO)

**Partnership Governance:**
- Formalize with MOU before proceeding
- RACI matrix prevents role confusion
- Tri-partite structure (City/CoStar/Community) distributes work effectively

**Intellectual Property:**
- "Civic Open Source Default" is critical for implementation
- City Legal review MUST happen pre-event
- CoStar employee IP waiver required

**Diversity & Inclusion:**
- 40% female/non-binary participation target is achievable with intentional effort
- "Clever marketing" > competitive language
- Judging criteria must value non-technical contributions
- Community Ambassadors reduce intimidation

**Challenge Design:**
- "Micro-Problem, Macro-Impact" principle
- 10-hour scoped challenges only
- Implementation Champion MUST be identified pre-event
- Non-technical challenges (data, design, policy) increase inclusivity

**Post-Event Implementation:**
- 6-month Implementation Task Force is essential
- Micro-grants ($1K-$5K) bridge the "Valley of Death"
- CoStar mentorship (2 hrs/week) stabilizes prototypes

---

### From Similar Events

**Registration:**
- Open registration 8-10 weeks before event
- Send weekly reminder emails as event approaches
- Expect 20-30% no-show rate (plan accordingly)

**Team Formation:**
- 50/50 split: pre-formed teams vs. individual matching
- Reserve 1-2 hours at start for team formation
- "Team canvas" activity helps new teams align quickly

**Mentorship:**
- Mentor-to-team ratio: 1:3 to 1:5
- Mentors should circulate, not wait to be asked
- Brief mentors thoroughly on challenges beforehand

**Judging:**
- 5-7 judges ideal (odd number for tie-breaking)
- 3-5 minute presentations + 2 min Q&A
- Rubric must be clear and shared with participants in advance

**Prizes:**
- Cash + tangible items (laptops, tablets) both work well
- Category awards increase winning opportunities
- "People's Choice" engages all participants in judging

---

### Things to Avoid

❌ Don't charge participants (kills diversity and inclusion)
❌ Don't rely on standard venue WiFi (will fail)
❌ Don't skip the IP/OSS conversation (blocks implementation)
❌ Don't let teams work on proprietary projects (defeats civic purpose)
❌ Don't over-order expensive catering (use weighted claim rates)
❌ Don't announce event without City Legal sign-off on OSS policy
❌ Don't skip contingency budget (always something unexpected)
❌ Don't forget accessibility (ADA compliance, dietary needs)
❌ Don't assume corporate venue feels welcoming (use Ambassadors)
❌ Don't end at the awards ceremony (implementation is the real goal)
