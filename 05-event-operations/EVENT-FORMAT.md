## 🎯 Format Philosophy

### The Challenge
Richmond needs a hackathon that:
- Gives teams real time to build quality solutions (not rushed 10-hour sprints)
- Creates the energy and momentum of overnight hacking culture
- Welcomes families and community to see what was built
- Maintains the authentic hackathon experience

### Our Solution: 33-Hour Marathon + Public Celebration
A traditional overnight hackathon with civic focus:
- **Friday 9 AM → Saturday 6 PM:** 33 continuous hours of hacking
- **Sunday Morning:** Virtual judging (judges review via Zoom)
- **Sunday 3-7 PM:** Public awards banquet and celebration

### Key Benefits
✅ **Real Development Time:** 33 hours allows teams to build production-quality solutions  
✅ **Authentic Experience:** Overnight hacking is where breakthroughs happen  
✅ **Community Celebration:** Sunday banquet welcomes families and celebrates civic impact  
✅ **High Energy:** Continuous format creates momentum and camaraderie  
✅ **Quality Projects:** More time = better implementations for the City

## 🤝 Team Formation Strategies

### Strategy 1: Pre-Formed Teams (Recommended)
- Teams of 2-5 register together before event
- Arrive together Friday morning
- Coordinate sleep/work shifts if desired
- Higher success rate (MLH data: pre-formed teams 2x more likely to complete projects)

### Strategy 2: Team Formation Friday Morning
- Solo participants matched during 10:30 AM team formation
- Based on interests, skills, and challenge preferences
- Organizers facilitate icebreakers and matching process
- Most common at hackathons

### Strategy 3: Saturday Join-In-Progress
- Saturday-only participants join existing teams
- Friday teams can "recruit" Saturday additions
- Works best if team planned for this (saved tasks for Saturday arrivals)
- Creates mentorship opportunity

### Strategy 4: Remote Team Members
- In-person core + remote contributors allowed
- At least 2 team members must be in-person for full 33 hours
- Remote members can contribute code but limited interaction
- Must have in-person representative for demo/submission

## ⚠️ Challenges & Mitigations

### Challenge 1: Venue Capacity for 500 People
**Problem:** CoStar may not have space for 500 participants

**Mitigations:**
- **Immediate action:** Confirm CoStar capacity this week
- **Option A:** Use overflow venue (VCU or 1717) for 150-200 participants
- **Option B:** Cap registration at CoStar capacity, waitlist overflow
- **Option C:** Move entirely to VCU (can handle 500+), CoStar sponsors instead

**Decision needed by:** December 1, 2025

---

### Challenge 2: Overnight Safety & Security
**Problem:** 24/7 venue access with 500 people requires robust security

**Mitigations:**
- Professional security staff (4-6 people overnight)
- Badge check-in/out system
- Well-lit venue 24/7
- Volunteer presence throughout night
- Emergency procedures clearly posted
- First aid station staffed
- Code of Conduct enforcement team present

**Estimated cost:** $1,500 for overnight security

---

### Challenge 3: Volunteer Staffing (100-120 people needed)
**Problem:** Need large volunteer corps for 33-hour event

**Mitigations:**
- Shift system: No one works more than 12-15 hours
- Recruit from CoStar employees, VCU students, RVA Tech community
- Provide meals and stipends for overnight volunteers
- Create compelling volunteer roles (not just grunt work)
- Recognition program (swag, certificates, LinkedIn endorsements)

**Recruitment goal:** 60 volunteers by January, 120 by February

---

### Challenge 4: Participant Fatigue & Attrition
**Problem:** Not everyone will stay all 33 hours

**Mitigations:**
- Set expectation: "Come/go as needed, but venue always open"
- Quiet rest spaces for naps
- High-energy programming (midnight activities, 3 AM surprises)
- Keep food and caffeine flowing
- Celebrate milestones (midnight toast, sunrise photo, etc.)
- Typical overnight attendance: 80% Friday night, 50% at 3 AM, 70% Saturday morning

**Expected pattern:** 500 Friday morning → 400 overnight → 350 Saturday
