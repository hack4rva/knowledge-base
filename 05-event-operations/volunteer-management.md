# Volunteer Management

**Hack for RVA - Richmond Civic Hackathon**

**Target:** 40-50 volunteers across 3 days  
**No one works all 3 days - shift system required**

---

## 👥 Volunteer Roles & Needs

### Role Categories

**1. Registration & Check-In (8-10 people total)**
- Friday AM shift: 3 people (10-12 PM)
- Saturday AM shift: 3 people (9-11 AM)
- Sunday shift: 3 people (11 AM - 1 PM)

**Responsibilities:**
- Check IDs and registration confirmations
- Distribute badges and welcome packets
- Process waivers for those who didn't complete online
- Answer basic questions
- Direct participants to spaces

**2. Community Ambassadors (12-15 people total)**
- Friday: 4 people (10 AM - 5 PM shift)
- Saturday: 5 people (9 AM - 5 PM shift)
- Sunday: 4 people (12 PM - 4 PM shift)

**Responsibilities:**
- Welcome participants warmly
- Help navigate venue
- Answer basic questions
- Monitor for lost/confused participants
- Social media documentation (photos/videos)
- Be a friendly face

**3. Technical Support (4-6 people total)**
- Friday: 2 people (12 PM - 10 PM)
- Saturday: 2 people (1 PM - 10 PM)
- Sunday: 1 person (11 AM - 2 PM)

**Responsibilities:**
- Help with WiFi connections
- Troubleshoot device issues
- Assist with cloud account setup
- Support project submissions
- Monitor technical infrastructure

**4. Food Service Coordination (4-6 people total)**
- Friday: 2 people (11 AM - 8 PM)
- Saturday: 2 people (11 AM - 8 PM)
- Sunday: 2 people (11 AM - 2 PM)

**Responsibilities:**
- Coordinate with caterers
- Set up food stations
- Monitor dietary restriction accommodations
- Cleanup between meals
- Track food levels (order more if needed)

**5. Logistics Support (6-8 people total)**
- Friday AM: 3 people (7 AM - 2 PM)
- Saturday AM: 3 people (7:30 AM - 2 PM)
- Sunday Setup: 3 people (8 AM - 12 PM)
- Teardown: 5 people (4 PM - 6 PM Sunday)

**Responsibilities:**
- Venue setup and teardown
- Maintain cleanliness
- Restock supplies
- Signage placement
- Load-in/load-out support

**6. Photography/Media (2-3 people total)**
- Friday: 1 person (10 AM - 7 PM)
- Saturday: 1 person (12 PM - 7 PM)
- Sunday: 2 people (12 PM - 4 PM)

**Responsibilities:**
- Event photography
- Social media content
- Video clips
- Respect opt-out badges

## 📅 Volunteer Shift System

### Principle: Maximum 8-Hour Shifts

**No one should:**
- Work all 3 days
- Work more than 8 hours in one day (with exceptions for core staff)
- Miss opportunities to network/participate

### Sample Shift Structure

**Friday Shifts:**
- Morning (7 AM - 3 PM): Setup + early event
- Afternoon/Evening (2 PM - 10 PM): Event coverage + closing

**Saturday Shifts:**
- Morning (7:30 AM - 3:30 PM): Setup + early event
- Afternoon/Evening (3 PM - 10 PM): Event coverage + closing

**Sunday Shifts:**
- Morning (8 AM - 1 PM): Setup + public event opening
- Afternoon (12 PM - 4 PM): Public event + awards
- Teardown (3:30 PM - 6 PM): Cleanup crew

## 🎁 Volunteer Benefits

### What Volunteers Receive

**All Volunteers:**
- Special "Staff" badge
- Free meals during your shift
- Hack for RVA staff t-shirt
- Volunteer appreciation certificate
- Letter of recommendation (upon request)
- First access to register for Year 2

**Additional Benefits:**
- VIP networking access (if shift permits)
- Behind-the-scenes experience
- Direct access to Mayor and City leaders
- Professional development
- Community contribution
- Free Sunday lunch + awards (even if not working Sunday)

**Time Commitment Recognition:**
- 1 shift: Certificate
- 2 shifts: Certificate + letter of rec
- 3 shifts: Above + special recognition
- Core staff: Public thank you at awards ceremony

## 📝 Volunteer Responsibilities

### Before Event

- [ ] Complete volunteer application
- [ ] Attend at least 1 training session
- [ ] Read all provided materials (Run of Show, Code of Conduct)
- [ ] Confirm your shift(s)
- [ ] Provide emergency contact info
- [ ] Submit dietary restrictions
- [ ] Arrive on time and prepared

### During Event

- [ ] Wear your staff badge visibly
- [ ] Follow Run of Show and instructions
- [ ] Communicate with your team lead
- [ ] Report problems immediately
- [ ] Be friendly and helpful
- [ ] Follow Code of Conduct
- [ ] Take care of participants
- [ ] Represent event professionally

### After Event

- [ ] Complete volunteer feedback survey
- [ ] Return any borrowed equipment
- [ ] Submit photos/content if applicable
- [ ] Participate in debrief (optional)

## 🎯 Volunteer Management Tools

### Volunteer Database

**Track in Airtable/Notion:**
- Name, email, phone
- Role(s) assigned
- Shift(s) working
- Dietary restrictions
- Emergency contact
- T-shirt size
- Training session attended
- Background check status (if required)
- Status: Confirmed / Pending / Cancelled

### Day-Of Tracking

**Check-In System:**
- Volunteers check in 30 min before shift
- Receive briefing from lead
- Get assigned specific role/location
- Check out at end of shift

### Communication

**Before Event:**
- Email updates as needed
- Remind 1 week before
- Final details email 24 hours before

**During Event:**
- Staff Discord/Slack (#staff channels)
- Text group for urgent issues
- In-person huddles at shift changes
