## 🏥 Medical Emergencies

### Life-Threatening Emergency

**IF SOMEONE IS:**
- Unconscious or unresponsive
- Not breathing or gasping
- Having chest pain or heart attack symptoms
- Severe bleeding
- Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis)
- Seizure
- Serious injury

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:**

1. **CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY**
   - Give address: CoStar Headquarters, [full address TBD]
   - Describe emergency clearly
   - Stay on line with dispatcher

2. **Alert CoStar Security**
   - They can guide EMTs to location
   - May have first responders on-site faster

3. **Notify Ford or Crystal**
   - Call their cell immediately
   - Don't wait

4. **Start First Aid (if trained)**
   - CPR if not breathing
   - Stop severe bleeding (apply pressure)
   - Use AED if needed (location: [TBD])
   - EpiPen if anaphylaxis and person has one

5. **Clear the Area**
- Give responders space
- Keep crowd back
- Designate someone to meet EMTs at entrance

6. **Contact Emergency Contact**
- Use info from registration
- Inform of situation
- Provide updates

### Non-Life-Threatening Medical Issues

**For minor injuries or illness:**
- Headache, minor cuts, upset stomach, etc.

**Actions:**

1. **Direct to First Aid Station**
   - Location: [TBD]
   - Staffed by: [TBD - recruit nurse/EMT volunteer?]
   - Supplies: Band-aids, pain reliever, ice packs

2. **Assess Severity**
   - Can they continue participating?
   - Do they need to rest in quiet room?
   - Should they go home?

3. **Document Incident**
   - Fill out incident report form
   - Note time, person, what happened, action taken
   - Store with event records

4. **Follow-Up**
   - Check in with person 30-60 minutes later
   - Offer support as needed

### Known Medical Conditions

**Participants with disclosed conditions:**
- Allergies, diabetes, epilepsy, anxiety, etc.

**Preparation:**
- List flagged during registration
- First aid staff aware
- Quiet room available
- Emergency contacts on hand

## ⚡ Power Outage

### If Power Goes Out

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:**

1. **Stay Calm & Inform Participants**
   - Announce: "Power outage, please remain where you are"
   - Emergency lighting should activate
   - Use phone flashlights if needed

2. **Contact CoStar Facilities**
   - Report outage
   - Get estimated restoration time
   - Determine cause (building-wide vs. local)

3. **Assess Situation**
   - Can we continue with WiFi on backup power?
   - Is it safe to continue?
   - How long is estimated outage?

4. **Decide: Continue or Pause**

**If <30 minutes outage:**
- Announce break
- Provide snacks
- Resume when power restored

**If 30-60 minutes:**
- Extend deadline by outage length
- Move outdoor if weather permits and safe
- Use phone hotspots for critical work

**If >60 minutes or unsafe:**
- Pause event for the day
- Communicate next steps
- Extend Saturday/Sunday as needed
- Provide refunds for catering if necessary

## 😰 Mental Health Crisis

### If Participant is in Distress

**Signs:**
- Panic attack
- Severe anxiety
- Emotional breakdown
- Concerning statements about self-harm

**Actions:**

1. **Approach Calmly**
   - Ask: "Are you okay? How can I help?"
   - Don't minimize their feelings
   - Take them seriously

2. **Provide Safe Space**
   - Move to Quiet Room [Location TBD]
   - Offer water
   - Stay with them (don't leave alone)

3. **Contact Resources**
   - **988:** Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
   - **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741

4. **Get Help**
   - Notify Ford or Crystal
   - Call 911 if immediate danger to self/others
   - Don't try to handle alone

5. **Follow-Up**
   - Connect with mental health professional if on-site
   - Encourage to go home if needed
   - Check in later

## 🍔 Food Allergy Emergency

### Allergic Reaction

**Mild Reaction:**
- Itching, hives, mild swelling
- Give antihistamine if person has it
- Monitor closely
- Notify first aid station

**Severe Reaction (Anaphylaxis):**

**SIGNS:**
- Difficulty breathing
- Swelling of throat/tongue
- Severe hives or rash
- Dizziness or loss of consciousness
- Rapid pulse

**ACTIONS:**
1. **Call 911 IMMEDIATELY**
2. **Use EpiPen if person has one**
   - Inject into outer thigh
   - Hold 3 seconds
   - Can repeat after 5 min if no improvement
3. **Lay person down** (unless difficulty breathing - then sit up)
4. **Keep them calm** 
5. **Alert CoStar security and staff**
6. **Stay until EMTs arrive**

**Prevention:**
- All food clearly labeled with ingredients
- Separate preparation for allergen-free meals
- Ask caterer about cross-contamination prevention
- Encourage participants with severe allergies to bring own food

## 🎒 Lost Child (Sunday Public Event)

### If Child is Lost

**Actions:**

1. **Get description immediately**
   - Name, age, appearance, clothing
   - When last seen, where
   - Parent/guardian name and phone

2. **Lock down exits**
   - Staff at all exits
   - Check everyone leaving
   - Do not let child leave with non-parent

3. **Search systematically**
   - Divide venue into zones
   - Search all rooms, restrooms, hiding spots
   - Check with all staff and teams

4. **Announce**
   - PA system: "[Child's name], please come to registration desk"
   - Don't create panic

5. **If not found in 5 minutes:**
   - Call Richmond Police
   - Alert CoStar security
   - Continue search

6. **When found:**
   - Verify identity with parent
   - Reunite safely
   - Gentle reminder to parent about supervision

**Prevention:**
- Child wristbands with parent contact [TBD]
- Designated kids' area visible to parents
- Clear supervision expectations

### After Emergency Resolved:

   - What happened?
   - What went well?
   - What could improve?

   - Adjustments to schedule

   - Complete incident reports
   - Collect witness statements
   - Take photos if relevant

   - City (if affects partnership)
   - Sponsors (if affects their experience)

5. **Follow-up**
   - Check on affected individuals
   - Process insurance claims
   - Update procedures
   - Add to lessons learned
