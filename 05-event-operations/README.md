## 🏢 Venue Requirements

### Space Needs
- **Main hacking space:** 150-200 people with tables for collaboration
- **Registration area:** Separate entrance/check-in zone
- **Meal service area:** Buffet-style food service
- **Breakout rooms:** 3-5 small rooms for mentor sessions
- **Presentation area:** Auditorium or large room with A/V for 200+
- **Sponsor booths:** Space for 5-8 sponsor tables

### Technical Infrastructure
- **High-density WiFi:** 4 devices per participant (600-800 devices total)
- **Power:** 1 power strip per table minimum
- **A/V:** Projector, screen, microphone, sound system for keynotes
- **Backup internet:** Redundant connectivity for emergencies

### Accessibility
- ADA-compliant entrances and elevators
- Gender-neutral restrooms
- Accessible seating and workspaces
- Clear signage and wayfinding

### Meal Plan (7 meals over 10 hours)
- **12:00 PM:** Networking Lunch (90% claim rate) - 180 servings
- **3:00 PM:** Afternoon Snacks (60% claim rate) - 120 servings
- **5:00 PM:** Dinner (85% claim rate) - 170 servings

### Budget Considerations
- **Average cost:** $9 per meal per person
- **Blended claim rate:** 65%

### Dietary Requirements
- Vegetarian options (30% of orders)
- Vegan options (10% of orders)
- Gluten-free available
- Allergen labeling required
- Halal/Kosher upon request

## 💻 Technology Infrastructure

### Network Requirements
- **Bandwidth:** Gigabit fiber minimum
- **WiFi:** Enterprise-grade mesh network
- **Device capacity:** 800 concurrent devices
- **Vendor:** Specialized event networking (WiFiT, eTech Rentals)
- **Budget:** $5,000 fixed cost

### Power Requirements
- 1 power strip per table (6 outlets minimum)
- Backup power for critical systems
- Extension cords and adapters available

### A/V Equipment
- Main stage: Projector, screen, lapel mics, sound system
- Backup: Secondary projector and screen
- Recording: Video recording for highlights/documentation

## 🔒 Security & Access Control

### Access Management
- Pre-registration required (no walk-ins)
- Photo ID check at entrance
- Wristbands or badges for participants
- Separate credentials for mentors/judges/staff/sponsors

### CoStar Campus Security
- Collaborate with CoStar security team in advance
- Clear visitor access protocols
- Emergency evacuation procedures
- Transparent security measures (avoid intimidating participants)

### Safety & Emergency
- First aid station and trained personnel
- Emergency contact information collected
- Fire exits clearly marked
- CoStar emergency procedures review
